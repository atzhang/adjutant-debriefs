How it's counted, and the raw numbers

Net value is enemy value killed minus value lost to the enemy, minerals plus gas. Spent is what everything of that type cost, survivors included; for a player it is the game's own total at its last stats sample, upgrades included. A dash means the replay gave no figure. Direct kills understate deterrence and spell support; a Siege Tank behind the army can still be useful. Kill credit goes to the last hit, and Carrier kills are credited through their Interceptors, so casters and units that soften or zone get little. Your team's net is the opponents' loss.
