Limitations and shared-vision assumption
  • The six-map-unit distance is a conservative documented heuristic, not a verified sight radius or a lower bound.
  • Only ordinary, non-burrowed observed unit types count as observers; transports and uncertain cargo are excluded.
  • Tracker position batches are sparse and damage-selected, so they are not a census of every observer or scan.
  • No position is interpolated. Building placement is carried only while the building stays stationary and ownership is unchanged.
  • The evidence supports an inferred proximity opportunity only. It does not establish actual vision, shared vision, attention, intent, or causality.
  • Shared-team availability is an assumption for a team's buildings. It is reported separately from a player's own building evidence.
  • A building without a qualifying sample has unknown coverage, not proof that it was unseen.
  • A sampled building opportunity does not show its research, upgrades, production queue, or hidden contents.
  • Counter timing is descriptive. Before/after order does not imply that either side reacted, and same-frame order is unresolved.
  • Omitted: Player camera positions and scan use are not treated as opponent vision and are not extracted here.
  • Omitted: No fog-of-war trace, terrain, high-ground, pathing, occlusion, upgrade, queue, or hidden-research model is applied.
